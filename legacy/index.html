/ how I work

I keep AI out of the parts where plain rules are safer.

On the risk-triage system, fixed rules do the scoring. The AI model only handles the messy Arabic text, and it never decides a student's risk.

I add an AI check on the AI-written messages before a person approves them. Re-runs are cached, so a full run of about 71 model calls drops to about 5.
